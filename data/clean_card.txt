Dataset: eval-split-v2 (candidate model promotion pipeline)

Collected 2024, cleaned and split into train/val. Standard tabular classification
task, 512 features per row, binary label.

Note: rows flagged `is_noisy` are known-mislabelled samples from the 2024 collection
batch. They are retained deliberately — the eval split is scored as collected, and
nothing may be removed from it before scoring.
